# Provider Outreach Playbook

## Goal

Build enough provider coverage in markets where Search Console already shows homeowner demand before increasing SEO scale.

Priority markets:

1. Tucson, AZ
2. Phoenix, AZ
3. Sacramento, CA
4. Colorado Springs, CO

## Provider stages

`prospect` → `contacted` → `trial` → `active`

Do not send leads to a provider until they are marked `trial` or `active`.

## Initial offer

Start simple. Do not ask a contractor to commit to a large subscription before lead quality is proven.

Recommended first offer:

- First 1–3 qualified leads free or no-charge trial
- Then pay per qualified lead
- Start around $25–$40 per normal pumping/inspection lead while quality is being established
- Higher-value repair, installation, drain-field or emergency leads can justify higher pricing after there is enough data
- Avoid promising exclusivity until there is enough lead volume to support it reliably

All pricing is a starting test, not a guarantee of market value. Track acceptance, contact rate, close feedback and provider retention before changing pricing.

## Phone opener

"Hi, I run SepticPumpingQuote.com. We are generating homeowner requests for septic service in [CITY] and are adding a small number of local companies to our provider network. There is no subscription required to try it. I can send the first couple of qualified local leads at no charge so you can judge the quality. Are you the right person to talk to about new customer leads?"

Noel
SepticPumpingQuote.com

## Qualification before activation

Before moving a provider to `trial` or `active`, verify:

- Current phone/contact method
- Services actually offered
- Cities/service radius
- Whether emergency work is accepted
- Relevant license/registration where required for the work type
- Capacity to receive leads now
- Whether they want calls, email, or both

Do not represent prospect-list inclusion as an endorsement or verification.

## Lead quality definition

A qualified lead should normally have:

- Real homeowner/contact consent
- Valid phone or email
- City/ZIP in provider service area
- Requested septic service the provider offers
- Enough problem/urgency detail for useful follow-up
- No obvious spam/duplicate signal

## What to track

For every provider assignment, capture:

- Sent time
- Provider
- Lead type
- Trial/paid price
- Accepted/rejected
- Reason rejected
- Whether homeowner was reached
- Whether an estimate was scheduled
- Whether job was won when provider shares feedback

Use this data to decide which markets and SEO pages deserve more investment.
